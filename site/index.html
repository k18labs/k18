k18

This page is served by the thing it describes.

k18 turns a folder of static files into a bootable x86-64 HTTPS server. The whole system (boot code, network driver, TCP, TLS 1.3, HTTP) is about 14 KB of assembly, and the files come behind it in the same image. There is no operating system underneath: no kernel, no shell, no file system, nothing to log into.

What answered your request is a cloud server with two shared virtual processors and 4 GB of memory, running nothing but that image.

It is fast at what a site is mostly made of and a quarter faster than nginx at large files, it speaks TLS 1.3 only, it serves GET and HEAD and nothing else, and a change of content or certificate is a new image and a reboot. That is the design: clunky, limited, small, and hard to break into.

The source is at github.com/k18labs/k18. How the numbers were measured, and what they do not say, is in its bench/README.md.
